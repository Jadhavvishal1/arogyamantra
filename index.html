Panchabhoutik Chikitsa is a unique branch of Ayurveda pioneered by Late Vaidya A.V. Datar Shastri, using the five elements — Earth, Water, Fire, Air and Space — as the foundation of treatment. Unlike conventional approaches, it focuses on correcting Agni (digestive fire) and assessing one's Prakriti (constitution) to treat the root cause with minimum medicine dosage.

Panchakarma is safe when done under proper medical supervision. At Arogya Mantra, each protocol is personalised after thorough health assessment. Therapies like Abhyanga and Shirodhara are deeply pleasurable. Stronger therapies like Vaman are prescribed only when clinically indicated. Always inform us of any existing conditions or medications beforehand.

PCOD responds very well to our integrated approach — combining Panchabhoutik Chikitsa for hormonal balance, customised herbal medicines, and YCB Level 6 certified Yoga therapy. Patients have reported regulated cycles, weight management, improved fertility markers, and significantly better quality of life. Results vary per individual but our clinical track record is strong.

We offer natural and synthetic hair wigs, hair patches for men and women, toppers, extensions, and specially designed wigs for chemotherapy and alopecia patients. Bases come in cotton and synthetic options. Hair textures include straight, wavy, and curly in all natural and custom colors. Every wig is custom-fitted for a natural, comfortable, daily-life-friendly look. Quick makeover, painless, no scar, no stitch.
